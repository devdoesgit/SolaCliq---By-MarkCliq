How rooftop solar worksPanels make DC power, the inverter turns it into AC for your home, and the net meter sends extra to the grid.
PM Surya Ghar subsidy₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third. Up to ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above.
What size do I need?Roughly 1 kW for every ₹1,000 of monthly bill. A 3 to 5 kW system suits most homes.
How long does install take?About 2 to 4 weeks from survey to handover, including the net meter visit.
