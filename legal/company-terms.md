and **MarkCliq**, Khushi Enclave-3, Jhampur, Mohali, SAS Nagar, Punjab 140301, India ("MarkCliq"). They add to the

people. If the two conflict, these terms win for the Company. A signed

The person who registers the Company confirms they are authorised to
accept these terms for it.

## 1. The service

MarkCliq provides SolaCliq, the app and the admin website, for the
Company's admin, installers, sales agents and customers. MarkCliq does
not do any solar work, sell any system, or act for the Company with any
customer, DISCOM or government body.

## 2. Registration and approval

The Company gives true registration details (GSTIN, legal and trade
name, address, owner's contact). MarkCliq may check them, call the
Company, and approve or reject the registration at its discretion.

## 3. Fees

Fees, if any, are agreed separately in writing (an order, an invoice or
a pilot agreement), plus GST. During a free pilot no fees apply. Unpaid
fees 15 days after their due date allow MarkCliq to
suspend the Company's account after notice.

## 4. The Company's responsibilities

The Company is responsible for:

- **Its people.** Who it adds as admin, installer or sales agent, what
  they do in SolaCliq, and removing people who leave.
- **Its customers and their data.** The Company is the *Data Fiduciary*
  for the personal data it keeps about its customers and staff. It must
  give them the notice and get the consent the Digital Personal Data
  Protection Act, 2023 requires before their data goes into SolaCliq,
  keep proof of that consent, and handle their requests (access,
  correction, erasure, grievances). Its sales agents must only register
  customers who have agreed.
- **KYC documents.** Collecting only the documents the subsidy or net
  metering process needs. Where a process accepts it, the Company should
  use a masked Aadhaar (last 4 digits visible).
- **Its business.** Prices, quotes, installations, warranties,
  payments, refunds, subsidy applications and any dispute with a
  customer, worker or agent.
- **Lawful use.** Using SolaCliq lawfully and as the Terms of Use allow.

## 5. Data processing (MarkCliq as Data Processor)

For the Company's records (customers, staff, tasks, photos, expenses,
KYC documents, payments), MarkCliq is the Company's *Data Processor*.
MarkCliq will:

1. process that data only to provide SolaCliq to the Company, and as
   the Company instructs through SolaCliq or in writing, or as Indian
   law requires;
2. keep each Company's data walled off from every other company's,
   and protect it as the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) section 6
   describes;
3. let only MarkCliq people who need it access the data, under a duty
   of confidentiality; enter the Company's account for support only
   when the Company asks, for a limited time, logged;
4. use only the service providers listed in the Privacy Policy, under
   terms that protect the data, and tell the Company before adding a
   new one;
5. tell the Company without undue delay, and within 48 hours, after
   learning of a personal data breach affecting its data, with what is
   known, and help it meet its own duty to notify;
6. help the Company answer its customers' and staff's requests about
   their data;
7. follow the retention periods in the Privacy Policy, section 7,
   including deleting KYC documents as listed there;
8. when the Company stops using SolaCliq: on request within 30 days,
   give the Company an export of its records; then delete them within
   60 days, except what law requires to be kept. Backups
   expire on their normal cycle (14 days).

## 6. MarkCliq's own data

MarkCliq is the Data Fiduciary for account data (names, emails,
passwords, settings) and for quote requests made directly to MarkCliq.
MarkCliq does not use the Company's customer data for its own
marketing, does not sell it, and does not pass the Company's customers
to any other company.

confidential and uses it only for this agreement, except what is public,
already known, or required by law to be disclosed.
