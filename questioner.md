# SolaCliq: questions for RY Enterprises

Pilot vendor meeting. Answers here decide how the MVP is built, so write
them down as given, including "don't know" answers.

Date: ______  Attendees: ______

## 1. Their business today

- How many installs a month? How many leads a month?
- Residential, commercial, or both? On-grid, off-grid, hybrid?
- Which areas do they cover, and how far do teams travel?
- Team size: office staff, installers, agents?
- What do they use today to track customers, jobs and money (Excel, Google
  Sheets, WhatsApp groups, paper)? Can we import their existing customers?
- What language do workers and customers prefer in an app: English, Hindi,
  Odia?
- What phones do workers use (Android? roughly how old)? Is mobile data
  reliable on site?

## 2. Install stages

Proposed: lead → site survey → quote → subsidy paperwork → install → net
metering → service.

- Is anything missing or in the wrong order (e.g. loan approval, material
  dispatch, DISCOM inspection)?
- Who moves a customer from one stage to the next? ----------------------------------------------------------
- What has to be done or collected before each stage can be marked complete? ----------------------------------------------------------
- After install, what service do they offer (cleaning, AMC, complaints)?

## 3. Documents

- Which documents do they collect from customers (electricity bill, Aadhaar,
  property papers, bank details, photos)? At which stage?
- Who uploads them: office, agent, worker, or the customer?
- Which documents do they produce (quote, invoice, subsidy forms, net-metering
  agreement, warranty card)?
- How long do they need to keep documents?

## 4. Worker installation photos (camera + location)

- Which photos are required after install (panels, inverter, meter, earthing,
  customer standing with the system)?
- What photo and location proof do the PM Surya Ghar portal and the DISCOM
  ask for? Can our photos be used for those submissions?
- Is a photo taken away from the site a problem, or just something to review?
- Are photos needed at other stages too (site survey, material delivery)?

## 5. Worker expenses

- What do workers spend money on (travel/fuel, food, materials bought on site,
  tools, labour)?
- Are receipts required? Are there limits per day or per job?
- Who approves expenses, and how are workers reimbursed? How often?
- Are expenses tied to a specific job/customer, or general?

## 6. Agents and commission

- How many agents, and how do they bring customers today?
- How is commission worked out now (flat per install, % of system cost,
  per kW)? Does it vary by agent?
- When is commission earned (on booking, after install, after full payment)?
- Agents will enter their own commission and expenses for admin review: what
  proof should they attach?
- What happens if two agents claim the same customer?
- Agents will register customers in the app on site; the customer reads out
  a code sent to their email. **Do most customers have an email address
  they can open on the spot?** If not, what do they use (a family member's
  email, WhatsApp only)? Login is email-only until SMS is set up.
- **Is anyone both a worker and an agent for you** (installs and also brings
  customers)? The app assumes one role per person per vendor for now.

## 7. Referrals

- Do they want customers to refer other customers?
- Who gets a reward: the new customer, the referrer, or both?
- What kind of reward: a fixed amount off, a percentage off, cash, or free
  service?
- Who sets the amount, and is it the same for everyone?
- When does the reward apply (on signup, on booking, after install)?
- Any cap on how many referrals one customer can use?

## 8. What customers see

- What should a customer see in the app: stage, dates, cost breakdown,
  documents, photos of their install?
- Cost breakdown: which line items (panels, inverter, structure, wiring,
  labour, DISCOM/net-metering fees, GST)? Should the subsidy be shown?
- Should customers see what they have paid and what is still due? (We are not
  collecting payments, but we could record them.)
- Should customers be able to raise service complaints in the app?

## 9. Admin and approvals

- Who will use the admin dashboard? One admin or several, with different
  permissions?
- What needs admin approval: expenses, commission, stage changes, discounts,
  anything else?
- Which reports matter most (installs per month, money pending, agent
  performance, worker expenses)?

## 10. Pilot terms

- Who on their side is our day-to-day contact for feedback?
- When can they start using it, and with how many real customers?
- What would make them say the pilot worked?
